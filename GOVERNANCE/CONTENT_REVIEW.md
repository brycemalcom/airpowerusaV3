# Content Review

Last updated: 2026-05-08

Purpose: prevent unsupported claims, legal-sensitive copy, or public-company statements from entering production without review.

Final approval authority:

- Roger Pawson is the final approval authority for AirPower claims, technical claims, investor-sensitive claims, and public materials.

Approved source rule:

- Existing live website copy and owner-provided campaign materials can be treated as approved source material unless Roger Pawson or the owner says otherwise.
- New claims that are not already present in approved source material still require review.

## Review Levels

### Level 1: Standard Commercial Copy

Examples:

- General product positioning.
- Customer inquiry copy.
- Conference landing page copy that avoids technical specifics.
- Descriptive site navigation.

Review:

- Owner review recommended.
- Legal review usually not required unless claims become specific.

### Level 2: Technical Claims

Examples:

- Power output.
- Storage capacity.
- Runtime.
- Efficiency.
- Temperature output.
- Emissions.
- Replacement of diesel, grid power, HVAC, or cooling systems.
- Patents and commercialization status.

Review:

- Owner/team approval required.
- Source should be recorded.
- Use conservative qualifiers when proof is not ready.

### Level 3: Investor / Securities Copy

Examples:

- Reg D, Reg CF, offering terms, minimum investment, share price, accredited investor language.
- SEC filings.
- Investment opportunity copy.
- Revenue projections, market-size claims, expected returns.

Review:

- Owner/legal review required before production.
- Must be separated from customer marketing unless explicitly scoped.

### Level 4: Public-Company / CETI Copy

Examples:

- CETI announcements.
- OTC ticker references.
- Board appointments.
- Manufacturing/distribution rights.
- Licensing language.
- Merger/acquisition/relationship statements.
- Opportunity-size statements.

Review:

- Owner/legal/public-company communications review required.
- Prefer citing official releases or filings.

## General Checklist

Before publishing:

- Identify whether copy is customer, investor, public-company, technical, or mixed.
- Check whether every specific claim has a source.
- Avoid unsupported superlatives.
- Avoid implying guaranteed performance, revenue, adoption, funding, or regulatory outcomes.
- Avoid changing public-company relationship language casually.
- Keep investor CTAs and customer CTAs distinct.
- Confirm domain and contact email.
- Confirm page is linked only where intended.

## Data-Center Campaign Guardrails

Allowed conservative positioning unless stronger approval exists:

- "Power and cooling support."
- "Supplemental cooling benefit."
- "Cold-air byproduct."
- "Designed for data-center and high-density computing environments."
- "Integrated BESS support."
- "Closed-loop compressed-air power concept."

Avoid unless approved/source-backed:

- Stating the system replaces data-center HVAC.
- Stating exact cold-air temperature ranges.
- Stating exact power capacity, runtime, or efficiency.
- Stating guaranteed uptime or resilience.
- Stating production readiness beyond approved language.
- Mixing investor/offering copy into the data-center customer page.
