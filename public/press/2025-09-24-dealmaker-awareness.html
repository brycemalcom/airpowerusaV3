Air Power USA Announces Engagement of DealMaker Securities for Investor Awareness of a $25M SEC REG D 504(c) Filing

San Diego, California — September 24, 2025

Air Power USA today announced the engagement of DealMaker Securities to support investor awareness for the Company’s SEC Regulation D 504(c) offering to raise $25M for the introduction of its proprietary “Clean Energy from Thin Air” technology.

The Company’s revolutionary compressed air engine (CAE) platform delivers zero‑emission power solutions for a sustainable future, ranging from mobile units to industrial‑scale stations.
