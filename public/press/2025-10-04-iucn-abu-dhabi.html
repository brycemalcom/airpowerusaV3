Air Power USA VP Phil Plumley Invited to Speak at IUCN Conference in Abu Dhabi, Launching Air Power USA Technology to the UAE

San Diego, California — October 4, 2025

Air Power USA today announced that Senior VP of Marketing Phil Plumley has been invited to the IUCN Conference in Abu Dhabi this month and will be introducing the Air Power “Thin Air to Clean Energy” technology to the UAE for the premier unveiling of the company’s unique, global, sustainable zero‑emission energy system in the region.

The conference includes highly private gatherings and meetings with the seven royal families of the Emirates, each representing their respective emirate.

Following this, the next day includes meetings with delegations from multiple countries, beginning with France and then expanding further. After these initial discussions, Phil Plumley will engage with leading funding institutions, environmental development funds, and other key stakeholders across the Emirates and the Gulf region, including Qatar, Saudi Arabia, Bahrain, and beyond.

This event represents a significant opportunity. Groups we will be speaking with will not only be offered investment opportunities but also explore factory development and investment at scale—potentially in the range of EUR 100–200M+ for Air Power USA joint venture opportunities.
